3. HEADSSS Assessment

H — Home

Who do you live with? Any changes at home recently? How are things at home? Any conflict or abuse at home?

E — Education / Employment

Are you in school/college/work? How is school going? Any recent changes? Attendance issues? Academic performance?

A — Activities

What do you do for fun? Do you have friends? Have your interests changed recently? Do you feel isolated?

D — Drugs & Alcohol

Do you or your friends use alcohol or drugs? What substances? How often? How much? Any IV drug use?

D — Depression & Mental Health

Over the past 2 weeks (PHQ-A concepts): Little interest/pleasure? Feeling down/hopeless? Sleep problems? Low energy? Appetite changes? Feeling bad about yourself? Difficulty concentrating? Moving slowly/restless? Thoughts of self-harm/suicide?

5

S — Sexuality & Relationships

Are you in a relationship? Have you been sexually active? Do you feel safe in your relationships? Any concerns about sexual health? How do you feel about your gender/sexuality identity?

S — Suicide & Self-Harm HIGH PRIORITY

Have you had thoughts of harming yourself or ending your life? Have you done anything to hurt yourself? When did this happen? How? What were you thinking/feeling? Do you have a plan? Access to means? Intent to act?

Form of Self-Harm (Partners in Paediatrics 2025-28)
Assessment Coverage (per hospital guideline)

Remorse/regret · Who knows · Frequency · Risk from others · Stressors (bullying, bereavement, relationships, abuse, sexuality) · Drugs/alcohol · Education · Family/social · Support network · Child protection

Priority Referral Team (PRT) Criteria

Deliberate SH (overdose, self-strangulation, serious cuts) · Deliberate harm from substance misuse with SH intent · MH symptoms: depression/active suicidality, psychotic symptoms, aggression/severe agitation · Low weight anorexia (BMI <17.5 or rapid weight loss). If met, refer ASAP for same-day review.
